HOW IT WORKS

Scanning & Recording

The scanned documents can be uploaded directly to the CPC server, from here they are recorded onto Black and White film. Exposed material is then processed.

Storing

A carefully engineered storage facility ensures your data remains secure.

Retrieval

A CPC designed film scanning system enables swift document retrieval.

Fast. Reliable. Secure.

Each Paper on Film roll is 2000 feet long and has 32,000 individual frames. CPC can process 3 x 2000 foot rolls per hour which means it's possible to have one million frames available for paper storage in a 10-hour day.

  • CPC provides a safe, secure, long-lasting and cost-effective method of storing paper documents/images by transferring digital data onto 35mm black and white film.
  • Black and white film has a proven lifetime of 500 years.
  • The documents are scanned and transferred via our film recording systems to black and white film.
  • The film is processed in a darkroom environment through our film processors.
  • Film reels are placed into storage.
  • Individual images can be retrieved using our film scanning technology.
  • Film images can also be viewed by the naked eye using a lightbox and a magnifying glass.
